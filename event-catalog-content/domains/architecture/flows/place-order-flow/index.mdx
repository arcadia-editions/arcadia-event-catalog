id: "place-order-flow"
name: "Place Order Flow"
summary: "Place Order Flow\n *\n * Covers the full lifecycle of a customer order during\
  \ a limited edition drop,\n * from the moment the customer submits their intent\
  \ through payment capture and\n * shipping confirmation — or cancellation if any\
  \ step fails.\n *\n * Stock reservation happens before payment. In a scarcity-driven\
  \ drop, inventory\n * is the binding constraint. We hold the item first and charge\
  \ second.\n *\n * Payment runs in two phases. Authorization at checkout places a\
  \ hold on the\n * customer's funds without moving money. Capture happens later,\
  \ as soon as\n * fulfillment is scheduled (FulfillmentScheduled); the warehouse\
  \ may mark the\n * order ready to ship only after capture. If fulfillment fails\
  \ after\n * authorization but before capture, the hold is voided — no charge ever\
  \ appears\n * on the customer's statement.\n *\n * Transient payment errors are\
  \ retried a bounded number of times. A hard\n * decline from the processor is not\
  \ retried — it goes straight to cancellation.\n *\n * Reservation expiry is owned\
  \ by the Scheduler. If payment has not started ten\n * minutes after the order was\
  \ created, the stock is returned to the pool. Once\n * payment has started, payment\
  \ owns the end: at most 3 attempts of at most\n * 30 seconds each, with 5 and 15\
  \ seconds of backoff between them.\n *\n * Redelivery: every policy is a no-op,\
  \ with no event, when the aggregate is\n * already in a target state of that command."

Place Order Flow
 *
 * Covers the full lifecycle of a customer order during a limited edition drop,
 * from the moment the customer submits their intent through payment capture and
 * shipping confirmation — or cancellation if any step fails.
 *
 * Stock reservation happens before payment. In a scarcity-driven drop, inventory
 * is the binding constraint. We hold the item first and charge second.
 *
 * Payment runs in two phases. Authorization at checkout places a hold on the
 * customer's funds without moving money. Capture happens later, as soon as
 * fulfillment is scheduled (FulfillmentScheduled); the warehouse may mark the
 * order ready to ship only after capture. If fulfillment fails after
 * authorization but before capture, the hold is voided — no charge ever appears
 * on the customer's statement.
 *
 * Transient payment errors are retried a bounded number of times. A hard
 * decline from the processor is not retried — it goes straight to cancellation.
 *
 * Reservation expiry is owned by the Scheduler. If payment has not started ten
 * minutes after the order was created, the stock is returned to the pool. Once
 * payment has started, payment owns the end: at most 3 attempts of at most
 * 30 seconds each, with 5 and 15 seconds of backoff between them.
 *
 * Redelivery: every policy is a no-op, with no event, when the aggregate is
 * already in a target state of that command.
