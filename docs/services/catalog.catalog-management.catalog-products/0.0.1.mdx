## Description

Product Catalog is the bounded context that defines what Arcadia Editions sells.
It owns the commercial identity of each release, including the SKU, description,
pricing, edition size, launch readiness, and publication lifecycle.

It tells the rest of the platform what exists in the catalog, while leaving stock
reservation and scarcity control to Catalog Inventory.

## Scope

- Create and maintain product definitions
- Manage publication and retirement lifecycle
- Own catalog-facing product attributes such as title, description, price, and launch date
- Define inventory tracking mode metadata for each release

## Main domain elements

- &#x60;Product&#x60;: aggregate for the commercial definition of a release
- &#x60;ProductCatalogService&#x60;: commands for creating, updating, publishing, and retiring products
- &#x60;ProductPublished&#x60;: event announcing a release is available to downstream contexts
- &#x60;ProductRetired&#x60;: event announcing a release is no longer active in the catalog
# Catalog Products

## Overview

Catalog Products is the publishing home for the commercial definition of every
Arcadia Editions release. It gives each offering a clear, enduring identity and
establishes the information needed for people across the business to understand
what is being presented for sale. The service treats a product as more than a
listing: it is the considered expression of an edition&#x27;s title, character,
commercial proposition, and place in the catalogue.

This page describes the business capability represented by Catalog Products. It
is intended as a durable guide to the service&#x27;s purpose and language, rather
than a technical reference. The detailed contracts and artifacts in the
repository remain the source for precise integration and delivery information.

## Domain context

Catalog Products sits within the Catalog Management subdomain of the Catalog &amp;
Inventory domain. Catalog Management is concerned with defining the offer that
Arcadia Editions brings to market: the releases it curates, the qualities that
distinguish them, and the conditions under which they are presented as part of
the active catalogue.

For a limited-edition business, a product definition provides the stable
commercial frame around an edition. It captures the qualities that make a
release recognisable and marketable, while allowing the catalogue to remain
intentional as collections, seasons, and editorial priorities change. This
bounded context creates a common business understanding of each release without
turning the catalogue into a record of operational activity.

## Responsibilities

Catalog Products maintains the authoritative commercial definition of a
product. Its responsibilities include establishing product records, curating the
customer-facing information that explains an offering, and keeping that
information coherent throughout the product&#x27;s catalogue life.

It manages the progression from an internal product definition to an offering
that is suitable for publication, and later to a state in which it is no longer
presented as active. This includes stewardship of descriptive, pricing,
edition, and release-oriented information so that product material remains
meaningful, accurate, and consistent over time.

The service also records the inventory-tracking intent associated with a
release. That role is deliberately descriptive: it helps express how an
offering should be understood in the catalogue without taking ownership of the
day-to-day accounting of units. In this way, Catalog Products preserves the
business meaning of an edition while leaving operational concerns in their
proper place.

## Boundaries

Catalog Products owns the commercial identity and publication lifecycle of a
release. It does not determine the physical availability of copies, make
allocation decisions, maintain fulfilment activity, or carry out financial
processing. It is also not a content-management system for every possible
editorial asset; its focus is the product information needed to define and
present an offer coherently.

The boundary keeps catalogue decisions understandable and auditable. Changes to
how a product is named, described, positioned, priced, or presented belong here
when they affect the commercial definition. Matters concerned with operational
execution, customer interaction, or downstream administration remain outside
this service&#x27;s responsibility.

## Business value

By giving every release a dependable commercial definition, Catalog Products
helps Arcadia Editions present a catalogue that is clear, curated, and aligned
with its editorial standards. It reduces ambiguity around what an offering is
and how it should be represented, supporting confident decisions by commercial
and editorial teams.

The service also makes change more deliberate. A defined publication lifecycle
allows the organisation to prepare releases thoughtfully, present them at the
right moment, and retire them without losing the historical meaning of the
catalogue. The result is a more trustworthy product estate: one that can evolve
with the collection while keeping each edition&#x27;s commercial story intact.
