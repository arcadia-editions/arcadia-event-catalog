id: "place-order-flow"
name: "Place Order Flow"
summary: "Place Order Flow\n *\n * Covers the full lifecycle of a customer order during\
  \ a limited edition drop,\n * from the moment the customer submits their intent\
  \ through payment capture and\n * shipping confirmation — or cancellation if any\
  \ step fails.\n *\n * Stock reservation happens before payment. In a scarcity-driven\
  \ drop, inventory\n * is the binding constraint. We hold the item first and charge\
  \ second.\n *\n * Payment runs in two phases. Authorization at checkout places a\
  \ hold on the\n * customer's funds without moving money. Capture happens later,\
  \ at fulfillment\n * time, when the order is physically ready to ship. If fulfillment\
  \ fails after\n * authorization but before capture, the hold is voided — no charge\
  \ ever appears\n * on the customer's statement.\n *\n * Transient payment errors\
  \ are retried within the reservation window. A hard\n * decline from the processor\
  \ is not retried — it goes straight to cancellation.\n *\n * Reservation expiry\
  \ is owned by the Scheduler, not the payment service. If\n * payment is not resolved\
  \ within ten minutes of the order being created, the\n * Scheduler fires ReservationExpired\
  \ and the stock is returned to the pool."

Place Order Flow
 *
 * Covers the full lifecycle of a customer order during a limited edition drop,
 * from the moment the customer submits their intent through payment capture and
 * shipping confirmation — or cancellation if any step fails.
 *
 * Stock reservation happens before payment. In a scarcity-driven drop, inventory
 * is the binding constraint. We hold the item first and charge second.
 *
 * Payment runs in two phases. Authorization at checkout places a hold on the
 * customer's funds without moving money. Capture happens later, at fulfillment
 * time, when the order is physically ready to ship. If fulfillment fails after
 * authorization but before capture, the hold is voided — no charge ever appears
 * on the customer's statement.
 *
 * Transient payment errors are retried within the reservation window. A hard
 * decline from the processor is not retried — it goes straight to cancellation.
 *
 * Reservation expiry is owned by the Scheduler, not the payment service. If
 * payment is not resolved within ten minutes of the order being created, the
 * Scheduler fires ReservationExpired and the stock is returned to the pool.
